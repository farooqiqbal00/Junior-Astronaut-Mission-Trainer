SPACE WEATHER LAB

🛰️ NASA Solar Data

Sun Watch uses NASA's DONKI solar-flare information when the live service is available.

What is NASA DONKI?

DONKI is NASA's space-weather information system. It records events happening on the Sun and in space.

Solar Flare Classes

  • C-Class: Weaker flares — minimal radiation risk.
  • M-Class: Stronger flares — need 2+ shield cells.
  • X-Class: Strongest class — need 3+ shield cells.

Data source: NASA DONKI · Health guidance: NASA OSDR
